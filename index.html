Welcome to my world of Computational Condensed Matter Physics

I am a postdoctoral fellow in the Physics Department at Central Michigan University. My research centers on metal–sulfur battery modeling using computational methods, in collaboration with experimentalists studying lithium–sulfur batteries. During my doctoral work, I focused on novel material modeling and applications of 2D carbon nanomaterials for hydrogen storage and generation.

I am passionate about exploring the fundamental properties of materials — electronic, optical, vibrational, mechanical, and topological — and their applications in the hydrogen economy, catalysis, and battery research using computational and machine learning approaches.
