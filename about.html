Research Experience

My academic focus centers on theoretical physics inclined toward a computational approach. I conducted my doctoral research in computational condensed matter physics at IIT Bombay, advised by Prof. Alok Shukla and Prof. Brahmananda Chakraborty .

I am currently a postdoctoral research fellow at Central Michigan University, working on metal–sulfur batteries under the supervision of Prof. Veronica Barone .
